STATISTICAL TESTS — Student Dropout Risk 2026 (n = 100,000)
================================================================================
Methods
-------
- Welch's t-test: compares means of dropouts vs retained students for each
  numeric variable (does not assume equal variances).
- Cohen's d: standardized mean difference (0.2 small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large).
- Point-biserial r: correlation between the numeric variable and the binary
  dropout flag.
- Chi-square test of independence: for categorical/binary variables, with
  Cramer's V as the effect size (0.1 small, 0.3 medium, 0.5 large).
- Standardized logistic regression: all predictors scaled to mean 0 / SD 1 so
  coefficients are directly comparable as a risk ranking.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT FOR ANALYSTS
-----------------------------
With n = 100,000, even trivially small differences reach p < 0.001. Effect
sizes (d, r, V) — not p-values — are the honest basis for conclusions below.
Genuinely null results (gender, nationality, course type, international
status) are reported as nulls because their p-values exceed 0.05 AND their
effect sizes are near zero.
